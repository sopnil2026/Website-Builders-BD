# Google Business Profile — eligibility and owner setup

Last reviewed: 22 November 2026

## Important eligibility check

Google Business Profiles are not for every online brand. Google says a business must make in-person contact with customers during its stated hours. Online-only businesses that neither receive customers at a physical location nor visit customers in person are not eligible.

Before creating a profile, confirm that Website Builders BD genuinely:
- meets customers at a real business location during stated hours; or
- travels to customers / provides an eligible in-person service.

If the work is remote-only and there is no eligible in-person customer contact, do not create a Business Profile just for SEO.

Official eligibility guidance: https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036

## If the business is eligible

1. Sign in using the Google Account controlled by the business owner or an authorized representative.
2. Open https://www.google.com/business/ and search for an existing profile before creating a new one.
3. Use the real-world business name exactly as used on signage, documents, and customer communications: Website Builders BD. Do not add keyword phrases to the business name.
4. Choose the most accurate primary category that Google currently offers. Add only relevant secondary categories.
5. Enter the actual customer-facing address only if customers are served there. If this is a service-area business and customers are not served at the address, hide the address and list only the genuine service areas.
6. Add only accurate service areas, hours, contact details, and the canonical website URL: https://websitebuildersbd.shop/
7. Add genuine photos and a clear description of the actual services. Do not use stock photos as proof of a location or invent an office.
8. Complete whichever verification method Google offers for this business. Verification options vary.
9. Ask real customers for honest reviews. Do not buy reviews, offer rewards for positive reviews, or post reviews on customers' behalf.

Google address and service-area guidance:
- https://support.google.com/business/answer/2853879
- https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481

Google requires accurate representation of the business. A residential address should not be shown publicly if the business operates as a service-area business and does not serve customers at that address.
