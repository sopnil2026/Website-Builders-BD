## Keyword usage rules

1. Give each page one clear primary topic. Do not make every page target every phrase.
2. Use supporting phrases only where the visible page content genuinely answers that intent.
3. Keep headings readable and descriptive. Do not repeat keywords unnaturally.
4. Keep brand mentions consistent and do not add keywords to the official business name in Google Business Profile.
5. Do not create doorway pages for towns or services that the business does not actually serve.

## Measurement and refinement

After Search Console has collected enough data, open Performance → Search results. Review the Queries and Pages tabs, filter by Bangladesh where available, and compare 28-day and 3-month periods. Prioritize queries that already show relevant impressions and pages whose titles or content do not fully satisfy the intent.

Do not report a keyword as ranking or high-volume unless Search Console or a keyword research source confirms it.
